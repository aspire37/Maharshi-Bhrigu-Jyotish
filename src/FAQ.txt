1. Refund & Cancellation Policy
Q: Why are your services non-refundable?
A: Vedic astrology consultations and reports require significant time, energy, and preparation, including in-depth analysis of your birth chart before the actual meeting. Once a booking is confirmed, our resources are dedicated to your session, making it impossible to offer refunds. 
Q: Can I cancel my booking and get a refund?
A: No. Once a consultation is booked and payment is made, cancellations are not eligible for a refund. 
Q: What if I need to reschedule my session?
A: We understand that scheduling conflicts occur. You may reschedule your appointment by informing us at least 24–48 hours in advance. Rescheduling within 24 hours of the appointment may not be permitted or may incur a fee. 
Q: What happens if I miss my appointment?
A: Missed appointments (no-shows) are considered consumed services and are non-refundable. 
Q: Under what circumstances can I get a refund?
A: Refunds are only considered in exceptional, rare cases, such as: 
A technical error on our side prevented service delivery.
Duplicate payments were made for the same service.
The booking was canceled within 1 hour of booking AND work had not yet begun (at our discretion).

Q: What happens if I provide wrong birth details?
